The new start sounds had a tiny pop at the front. Like someone tapping the mic before a toast. Nobody asked for the tap. Everybody heard the tap.

It wasn't the sounds. We checked every one of them, and they start in perfect silence. It was the timing.

When you start dictating, Dictate opens your microphone, and your Mac takes that as a cue to rearrange its audio hardware. Headsets switch modes. Audio interfaces shuffle things around. Dictate was playing the sound the moment the mic said "I'm open," which is a bit like a microphone saying "I'm ready" while it's still being plugged in.

Now Dictate waits until real audio is coming in from the mic, gives the hardware a moment to settle, then plays the sound. You won't notice the wait. It's about a tenth of a second. You will notice the pop is gone.

The short version:

- Fixed: a pop or crackle at the start of the start sound when dictation begins.
- The sound now plays once the microphone is actually delivering audio.
