Remember last update, when we gave you fifteen gorgeous, warm, buttery new start sounds? Turns out every single one of them came with a free little *pop* on the front. Like your Mac cracked its knuckles. Or worse. Let's be honest, some of you thought it farted. It sounded like it farted. We're not going to pretend it didn't.

So we went looking for the culprit. We checked all fifteen sounds, sample by sample, like a raccoon going through a dumpster. Spotless. Every one starts in perfect silence. The sounds were innocent. The sounds were framed.

The real criminal was timing. When you start dictating, Dictate opens your microphone, and your Mac takes that as a cue to rip its whole audio setup apart and put it back together. Headsets switch modes. Audio interfaces panic. And Dictate, like an absolute dumbass, played the sound right in the middle of the chaos. It was like starting your wedding speech while the DJ is still plugging in the speakers. *Thhbbpt.* Hello everyone.

Now Dictate waits for the mic's hardware to actually be running, then plays the sound. The wait is a few milliseconds. You blink slower than that. The mic itself opens exactly as fast as it always did, so you're not waiting one crumb longer to start talking. The only thing that waits is the sound, which has learned some damn manners.

No more pop. No more crackle. No more "did my laptop just break wind at me." Just a clean little boop and then you, talking.

The short version:

- Fixed: a pop or crackle at the start of the start sound when dictation begins.
- The sound now plays once the mic hardware is running, a few milliseconds in. Recording starts at the same moment as before.
