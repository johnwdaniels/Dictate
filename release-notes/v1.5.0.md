You know when you're emailing someone named Siobhan, and you say "Siobhan," and the speech model writes "Shivon"? And now you've misspelled someone's name right to their face? Mortifying. And that was on us.

Dictate now reads the names in the window you're typing into and spells them the way they're shown. Siobhan stays Siobhan. Diplomacy restored. We read the window's text, not a picture of it. No screenshots. You can turn it off in Settings.

Also, a dictation that lands in the middle of a sentence now starts lowercase, instead of Randomly Capitalizing Like A Ransom Note.

And Hold or tap is the default now. Hold the shortcut to talk, or tap to start and tap again to stop. It's a buffet. Go nuts.

The short version:

- Names are spelled the way they appear on screen. You can turn this off in Settings.
- A dictation landing mid-sentence starts lowercase.
- Hold or tap is the default shortcut mode.
