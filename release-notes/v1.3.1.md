The live text in the pill had the memory of a goldfish. You'd finish a phrase and it would wipe the slate and start over, like every sentence was its first day on Earth.

We built that. On purpose, kind of. It was dumb.

Now finished phrases stay put and new words stream on after them, so you see one growing transcript instead of a flickering amnesiac.

The short version:

- Live text in the pill keeps finished phrases and grows as you talk, instead of resetting.
