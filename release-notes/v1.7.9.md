Remember an hour ago when we said the pill was proper glass now? It was proper glass the way a bathroom window is proper glass. Smoky. Private. Great for a shower, less great for showing off.

So we turned the smoke down by half. The pill now lets a lot more of whatever's behind it come through. Album art, your wallpaper, the cat video you're definitely not watching at work, it all glows through the glass.

The catch with see-through is white web pages. Turn the smoke down too far and the white words disappear into a white Google Doc like a polar bear in a snowstorm. So the words and the waveform now have a soft shadow under them, the same trick your iPhone Lock Screen uses so the clock doesn't vanish into a bright wallpaper. We tried this on Wikipedia, GitHub, Apple's own website and a beach wallpaper before shipping it, because we've learned our lesson. Mostly.

The short version:

- The dictation pill is more see-through: half the dimming it had in 1.7.8.
- A soft shadow under the words and waveform keeps them readable over white pages.
- Same shape, same motion, same lit glass edges.
