The little pill that pops up while you talk got a glow-up. We stared at the player bar in Apple Music for an embarrassingly long time, and it's gorgeous. Ours looked like a frosted ice cube someone forgot in the freezer.

So now the pill is proper glass. The edges catch the light, brightest at the top left and bottom right, the way real glass does when a lamp hits it. The glass is dark and smoky, like Music's, even if your Mac is in light mode. And there's a little dimming behind the words, so they stay readable over a blinding white web page too.

Fun fact we learned the hard way: macOS only lets glass bend and reflect stuff inside its own window. The pill floats over other apps, so the system can only blur them. Doing the real thing would mean asking you for screen recording and putting a recording light in your menu bar every time you talk. Hard pass. So we drew the reflections ourselves, like a kid adding a shine mark to a drawing of an apple, except it actually looks good.

The pill is the same shape and moves the same way. It's just prettier now. Like us after a haircut, in theory.

The short version:

- The dictation pill has lit glass edges, like the player bar in Apple Music.
- It's dark glass in light and dark mode, with a dimming layer so the words stay readable over white pages.
- Same shape, same motion, nothing new to set up.
