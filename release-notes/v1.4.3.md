New, and off unless you turn it on: AI editing.

Switch it on and, after you stop talking, a language model tidies up the transcript. Grammar, dropped words, obvious mishearings. Say "let's meet Tuesday, no, Wednesday" and it writes "let's meet Wednesday," like you had your shit together all along.

But it's off by default, because it adds a little time, and this app is about speed. It's a friend who proofreads your texts. Helpful. A bit slow. Totally optional. And if the call fails, you just get your original words back, untouched.

The short version:

- New optional AI editing that repairs a finished transcript. Off by default.
- If it fails, you get your original text.
