Remember two updates ago, when we swore up and down we'd fixed the pop? We had not fixed the pop. We'd fixed *a* pop. The pop's cousin kept showing up, specifically when you dictated a few times in a row, like a fart that waits until the second date.

This time we didn't guess. We hooked a recorder up to the actual speaker feed, fired off dictation after dictation like lunatics, and went through the recording sample by sample. Hundreds of boops. Our coworkers think we've lost it. They're not wrong.

Here's the crime. Every time a dictation ended, Dictate shut the microphone off. Every time a new one started, it switched it back on. Some mic setups really, *really* hate being switched back on a second after you switched them off. They throw a tiny tantrum, and that tantrum leaks straight into your speakers, right on top of the start sound. *Pfft.* We'd been flicking the light switch on and off like a toddler and blaming the bulb.

So now the mic stays on for five seconds after you finish, listening to absolutely nothing. It throws that audio straight in the trash, unheard and unsent. If you start talking again inside those five seconds, Dictate just picks the mic back up, already warm. No restart, no tantrum, no pop. And back-to-back dictations start faster too, because there's nothing to switch on.

You'll see the orange mic dot in your menu bar hang around about five seconds longer than before. That's the mic idling, not listening. Pinky promise.

The short version:

- Fixed: a pop at the start of the start sound when dictating several times in a row.
- The microphone stays open for 5 seconds after a dictation, with its audio discarded, so a quick next dictation reuses it.
- Back-to-back dictations start faster.
- The orange microphone indicator stays on about 5 seconds after a dictation ends.
