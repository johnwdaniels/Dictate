Dictate's start sound used to be a ding.

A ding. Like a microwave. Every time you started talking, your Mac announced that your burrito was ready. There was no burrito. There was only you, about to say something important, and a kitchen appliance cheering you on.

So we threw out all five old sounds. Ding, Boop, Pop, Chime and Tap. Gone. They were made of math, and you could tell.

There are fifteen new ones. Warm, round, short. Boops, dots, a little bubble, a wooden knock, a robot chirp, one that fades in like the start of a movie. None of them is a ping. We checked. We checked a lot. Pick one in **Settings → While dictating → Start sound**, and choosing a sound plays it, so you can audition all fifteen like a very small talent show.

The names are inside jokes. You'll get some of them. We'll get all of them. That's how inside jokes work.

If you'd picked one of the old sounds, Dictate switches you to the new default when it opens. You didn't do anything wrong. Your sound just retired.

Now, the other thing. Some of you saw a notification that said a prompt contained a **disallowed character**, and then your dictation just didn't happen. You talked. Nothing came out. Very spooky.

Here's what was going on. Dictate reads a few unusual words off your screen, names mostly, so it spells them the way your screen does. Your Mac draws apostrophes curly, like this: ’. The speech service only accepts the straight kind. So one "haven’t" sitting somewhere on your screen was enough to cancel the whole dictation. Over an apostrophe. A comma with ambition.

Now Dictate straightens those apostrophes before it sends anything. And if a word still has a character the service won't take, Dictate leaves that one word out instead of the whole dictation. Worst case, one name gets spelled a little wrong. Best case, you never think about apostrophes again. We certainly plan not to.

The short version:

- Fifteen new start sounds replace Ding, Boop, Pop, Chime and Tap. Pick one in Settings → While dictating → Start sound.
- If you'd chosen an old sound, you move to the new default.
- Fixed: a curly apostrophe on screen could make dictation fail with a "disallowed character" error.
