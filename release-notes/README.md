# Release notes

Each release reads its notes from `<tag>.md` in this folder, such as `v1.5.5.md`. Commit the file before pushing the tag.

## The voice

The notes are a short stand-up set. A comedian is on stage, telling people what changed in Dictate.

- Every bit lands on a fact: what changed, what the user notices, and anything they have to do, such as a setting to turn on.
- Open with the problem the way a person felt it, then the reveal, then the fix.
- The joke is on the bug, the computer, Bluetooth, or us. Never on the user.
- Clean. No jokes about real people, and no names of people who reported something unless they said yes.
- Short. A few paragraphs per change. If a bit needs explaining, cut it.
- End with **The short version:**, a plain list of what changed, for anyone who skipped the set.
