# Release notes

Each release reads its notes from `<tag>.md` in this folder, such as `v1.5.5.md`. Commit the file before pushing the tag. The same files ship inside the app and show on its Release Notes page, newest first. Paragraphs, `- ` bullets, **bold** and *italics* show there; headings and links do not.

## The voice

The notes are a short, crude stand-up set. A comedian who has had two drinks is on stage, telling people what changed in Dictate. `v1.7.1.md` is the level to match.

- Every bit lands on a fact: what changed, what the user notices, and anything they have to do, such as a setting to turn on.
- Open with the problem the way a person felt it, then the reveal, then the fix.
- The joke is on the bug, the computer, Bluetooth, or us. Never on the user.
- Self-deprecating is the sweet spot. We wrote the bug, we're idiots, we fixed it.
- Crude is welcome: swearing, potty humor, your Mac breaking wind. No sexual jokes, no slurs, nothing mean.
- No real people's names or jokes about them, including whoever reported something, unless they said yes.
- Short. A few paragraphs per change. If a bit needs explaining, cut it.
- End with **The short version:**, a plain list of what changed, for anyone who skipped the set. Keep that list clean and literal.
