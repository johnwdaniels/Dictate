You'd be mid-thought in Messages, hit the shortcut, and Dictator would open your text with "Start FaceTime." Like a butler announcing a guest nobody invited. Then the rest of what you actually said would show up behind it.

Here's the embarrassing part: we did that. To spell names right, Dictator reads what's on your screen and hands Inworld a short list of words to listen for. Our reader was a nosy idiot. It didn't just read your chats, it read every app's menus too: "Start FaceTime", "FaceTime Audio", "Force Quit", "Log Out". Then it handed all that to Inworld as if those were names you might say. In a quiet moment before you start talking, the model sometimes reads the list back. So you got a menu command as the first line of your text.

It was also picking up chat filler as "names." Lol. Hahaha. Lmao. Sooooooo. The spell checker had never heard of them, so our reader figured they must be people. The Messages hints for one dictation today read like a group chat having a breakdown.

Now the reader skips menus entirely, never builds a hint that starts with a button word like Start, Join or Call, and throws out laughs, lols and stretched-out words before anything leaves your Mac. Real names in your chats still go through, so your friends' names still get spelled right.

The short version:

- Fixed: a menu label such as "Start FaceTime" could show up at the start of a dictation.
- Screen hints no longer include menu items from any app.
- Screen hints never start with an action word such as Start, Join or Call.
- Chat filler such as lol, haha, lmao and stretched words like "Sooooooo" is no longer sent as a hint.
- Names on screen still go out as hints, as before. Nothing to set up.
