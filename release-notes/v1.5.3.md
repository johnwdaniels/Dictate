To help the speech model spell your words right, Dictate quietly passes it a list of expected terms. Your names. Your jargon.

Sometimes, when you paused, the model would read the list back. "Expected terms: Kubernetes, Siobhan, quarterly." Right into your email. Like a news anchor reading the teleprompter notes on live TV. "Look sad here."

We now catch those echoes and throw them out, even the ones that get cut off halfway. But if you actually say "expected terms" yourself, for whatever weird reason, we keep it. You're the boss.

The short version:

- Echoes of Dictate's term list, like "Expected terms: …", are removed from transcripts, even when cut off.
- If you really say "expected terms," it stays.
