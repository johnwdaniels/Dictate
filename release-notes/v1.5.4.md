So, the start sound. The little ding. It's supposed to go *ding.*

For some of you it was going *di— ding.*

Like it saw something. Like the chime got halfway through, had a thought, and started over.

Here's what was happening. We played the chime, and a few milliseconds later we turned on the microphone. And if your headphones are also your microphone, which is AirPods and basically every headset, turning on the mic makes macOS restart the whole audio device. Mid-ding. The chime gets cut off, the device comes back, and it dings again.

It's not haunted. It's Bluetooth. Which, to be fair, is a little haunted.

So now the mic turns on first, and *then* the chime plays. The pill still pops up the instant you press the key. The sound just waits until we're actually listening, which, honestly, is what a ding should mean. The ding is a promise now.

The short version:

- The start sound plays once the microphone is on, so headsets no longer cut it off and replay it.
- The sound can come a few milliseconds later than before. On AirPods, the first one after a while can take a bit longer while they switch into mic mode.
- With the start sound set to Off, nothing changed.
