You used to update Dictate by hand. Go to GitHub. Download a zip. Drag it into Applications. Like it was 2009 and we were all burning mix CDs and lying about it.

Now Dictate checks for updates once a day and offers to install them. Want it right now? Check for Updates is in the menu bar.

And when you click Open Dictate, the window actually comes to the front now. Before, it would open politely behind everything else, like someone who shows up late to a meeting and sits in the hallway.

The short version:

- Dictate checks for updates once a day, and Check for Updates is in the menu bar.
- The Dictate window comes to the front when opened from the menu bar.
