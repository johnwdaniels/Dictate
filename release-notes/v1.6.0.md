Dictate got a makeover.

You know how some apps look like they were designed by a spreadsheet? Gray box. Gray box. Slightly darker gray box, for emphasis. We were one of those apps. We're not proud of it.

So now the window is glass. Actual see-through glass. Your desktop shows through behind it, blurry and flattering, like a dating profile photo.

**Settings** got the treatment too. Every setting has its own little colored icon now, a bold name, and what it does written right underneath. No more reading a toggle, then reading the sentence two lines below it, then going back up to figure out which toggle that sentence belonged to. They live together now. They're very happy.

**History** shows which app each dictation went into, with that app's icon. So when you're hunting for the thing you said in Slack, you're looking for the Slack icon, not reading forty paragraphs of yourself. Nobody wants to read forty paragraphs of themselves. Trust us, we've tried.

And **Copy** is a real button now. Before, it was the word "Copy," just sort of standing there. Was it a button? Was it a label? Was it a suggestion? It was a button. It was always a button. Now it looks like one.

The recording pill kept its shape, because it was already perfect, and we don't mess with perfect. It's on clearer glass now. Same pill. Better lighting.

There's a new **Release Notes** page in the sidebar, under Settings. You're reading it right now, probably. Hi.

Which brings us to **Debug.** Debug is gone. It was a page for testing the speech model by hand, and it kept its own copy of your API key, like a roommate who made a spare key without asking. The page is gone, and Dictate deletes that spare copy the first time it opens. Your real key is fine. It never left.

And if you scroll all the way to the bottom of Settings, there's a little note from the two of us. With a heart. It beats. We're not saying it's the best feature. We're not *not* saying it.

The short version:

- The window is see-through glass on macOS 15 and later.
- Settings rows have icons, bold names, and their explanation underneath.
- History shows the icon and name of the app each dictation went into, and Copy is a proper button.
- The recording pill keeps its look on clearer glass.
- New Release Notes page in the sidebar.
- The Debug page is gone, and Dictate deletes the spare API key copy it kept. Your real key stays where it is.
