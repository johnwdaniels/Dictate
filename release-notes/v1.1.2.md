So when you hesitate before talking, the speech model sometimes writes 嗯.

That's Chinese for "mm." You did not say anything in Chinese. You said nothing. You took a breath. And the model went, "A breath! I'll translate that." Like a waiter who brings you soup because you sighed near the menu.

We now quietly delete a lone 嗯 or one of its little friends when it shows up at the start or end of an otherwise English dictation. Your breathing stays private. As God intended.

Also, if an update makes macOS forget that Dictate is allowed to paste, we now actually tell you how to turn Accessibility back on, instead of silently not pasting and letting you stare at an empty text box like an idiot. Sorry. That was us being the idiot.

The short version:

- A stray 嗯 that the model writes for a hesitation is removed.
- Clearer help for turning Accessibility back on after an update.
