When a dictation came back empty, we used to just give you nothing. No text. No explanation. Like a cat bringing you an invisible dead mouse and walking away proud.

Now we tell you what actually went wrong:

- "Couldn't reach Inworld." The connection never got an answer. Usually a VPN or firewall sitting on it like a fat pigeon.
- "The microphone sent only silence." Check which microphone is chosen in Settings. It may be listening to absolutely nothing. Very zen. Very useless.
- "No speech was transcribed." We heard sound, but no words. Maybe you sneezed. Bless you.

Also, Test connection used to hang forever when something was broken. Now it gives up after five seconds and tells you what it found. We gave it a curfew.

The short version:

- Empty dictations now say why: no connection, a silent microphone, or no words heard.
- Test connection stops after five seconds instead of hanging.
