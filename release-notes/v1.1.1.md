Look down at your keyboard. Right side, next to the arrows. That's Right Option.

When's the last time you pressed Right Option on purpose? Never. Nobody has. It's been sitting there since the eighties like the appendix of keyboards. Useless. Probably fine. Might explode one day.

Well, Right Option, your phone's ringing. You can now use a single right-side modifier on its own, like Right Option or Right Command, as the dictation shortcut. No combo, no finger yoga. Just the one key, finally earning its keep.

The short version:

- A right-side modifier on its own, such as Right Option, can now be the dictation shortcut. Set it in Settings.
